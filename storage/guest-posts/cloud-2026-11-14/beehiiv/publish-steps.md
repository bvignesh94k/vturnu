## Steps

1. In app.beehiiv.com, open your publication and choose **Start writing** (or Posts, then New post).
2. **Title:** Google's November Core Update Is Rolling Out. Here Is a Calm Plan for Your Website
3. **Subtitle:** What Google has confirmed, what to leave alone, and the one comparison to run once it finishes.
4. **Body:** paste everything from "On 10 November 2026..." down to the italic bio line. Keep the section titles as Heading 2, the bullet list as bullets and the four jobs as a numbered list.
5. **Links:** check these four survive the paste (select the words and press Ctrl+K if not):
   - "Google Search Status Dashboard" → https://status.search.google.com/incidents/WLxcJyC6jMWMfryxsKMc
   - "guide to core updates" → https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates
   - "guide to debugging search traffic drops" → https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops
   - "November 2026 core update page" → https://vturnu.com/updates/november-core-update-2026/ (your one vturnu.com link)
6. **Thumbnail:** upload beehiiv-november-core-update-calm-plan.jpg. Alt text: Banner reading "The core update is rolling out. Stay calm." with four steps: note the dates, save a baseline, change nothing big, compare like with like.
7. **Web tab, SEO settings:**
   - Meta title: Google November 2026 Core Update: A Calm Plan for Your Site
   - Meta description: Google's November 2026 core update started on 10 November. What is confirmed, what not to do during the rollout and what to check after.
   - URL slug: google-november-2026-core-update-calm-plan
   - Content tags: SEO, Google updates, B2B marketing
8. **Audience:** publish to the web. Send it by email only to people who subscribed to your publication (beehiiv's policy forbids emailing anyone who did not opt in).
9. **Publish.**

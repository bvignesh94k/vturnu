# Owner's standing rules for guest posting (added 14 and 15 Nov 2026)

These come from B. Vignesh's messages during the cloud run. They sit on top of the rules in the original brief.

## Content
- Never write about VTurnU's clients: no client names, no client results, no case studies, no "in client audits". This covers articles, pitches, bios and social posts.
- Never mention sites where his articles are already published (no "as seen on", no list of earlier guest posts) in articles, bios or pitches. If a site asks for writing samples, use guides from the VTurnU blog.
- Write practical, high-quality articles that stand on public research and clear how-to steps.

## Bio
- Only digital marketing, the company's founding history and his career.
- Never mention Odoo, Salesforce, SAP, ERP, CRM implementation, IT consulting or IT staffing.
- Facts allowed: career in digital marketing began in 2017; started VTurnU in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic; incorporated in 2025; based in Chennai, India; Founder & CEO.

## Emails
- When he asks to send links, write every link in the email body itself, each with a short label. An attachment can be an extra copy, never the only place. (On 14 Nov the SIIT share links went only in an attached note, and he had to send them again himself.)
- Known limit: the Gmail connector routes any link in the body through a Google redirect page. The link text shown is still the real address. Links inside attachments and in the Gmail signature stay direct.
- Paid offers: answer "We are not interested in buying links or paid placements. We just want to share high-quality content that engages your audience."
- Founder features (CEO and founder magazines): his wording is "We are a startup, looking to improve our brand VTurnU ... please help us to publish about our founder and CEO. We will add the link with dofollow on our website and share it on our social media. Please help startups like us."
- Never pay, never swap links between third-party sites, never hide that VTurnU is his company.

## Platforms he posts on himself (local session with his logged-in Chrome)
- beehiiv, Hashnode (vturnu.hashnode.dev) and Indie Hackers (@bvignesh). The cloud session cannot log in to these.

## Speed and new sites (added 15 Nov, evening)
- He wants published results quickly: at least two guest blogs live by 16 Nov, and five in all.
- Only high-quality sites that have never been contacted. Check tracker-rows.csv and the exclusion list in the original brief before any new pitch.
- Prefer sites that take a complete article by email and publish contributed pieces most days. Send the full package in one email: article, cover image, photo, bio.
- Never promise him a publication date. Editors decide. Say plainly what is in his own control.

## AI wording (added 15 Nov, evening)
- No article, bio or email says it was written with AI help. That stays the rule unless a site's own rules require a disclosure; in that case the site is skipped or the disclosure is made.
- Sites that ban AI-written articles (HubPages, Techpoint Africa, APMdigest, IndustryWeek and similar) are never sent a drafted article, and text is never reworked to slip past their checks. For HubPages the owner writes the article himself from an outline (hubpages/writing-kit.md).
- Natural writing in his own voice is the standard everywhere.

## Client website links (added 16 Nov, afternoon)
The owner wrote: "if they allow - Kindly try to add my client websites - saiimpression.com, cafy.careers, firstchoiceautopart.com, groviya.com, zealitdigital.com - Not mandatory ... Never Ever mandatorily inject. vturnu.com link is only mandatory."
- A vturnu.com link is required in every piece (in the body where the site allows it, otherwise in the bio).
- A client link is optional. Use one only when the publisher's guidelines allow outside links and a page on the client's site genuinely helps the reader with the point being made. Never add one just to place a link.
- If a client page is linked, say plainly that the site is a VTurnU client, so the relationship is not hidden. The 14 Nov rule still applies otherwise: no client results or case studies unless the owner supplies them.
- Skip client links where the site allows only one author link, asks for vendor-neutral copy, or bans promotional links.

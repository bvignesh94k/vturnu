# Owner's standing rules for guest posting (added 14 and 15 Nov 2026)

These come from B. Vignesh's messages during the cloud run. They sit on top of the rules in the original brief.

## Content
- Never write about VTurnU's clients: no client names, no client results, no case studies, no "in client audits". This covers articles, pitches, bios and social posts.
- Never mention sites where his articles are already published (no "as seen on", no list of earlier guest posts) in articles, bios or pitches. If a site asks for writing samples, use guides from the VTurnU blog.
- Write practical, high-quality articles that stand on public research and clear how-to steps.

## Bio
- Only digital marketing, the company's founding history and his career.
- Never mention Odoo, Salesforce, SAP, ERP, CRM implementation, IT consulting or IT staffing.
- Facts allowed: career in digital marketing began in 2017; started VTurnU in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic; incorporated in 2025; based in Chennai, India; Founder & CEO.

## Emails
- When he asks to send links, write every link in the email body itself, each with a short label. An attachment can be an extra copy, never the only place. (On 14 Nov the SIIT share links went only in an attached note, and he had to send them again himself.)
- Known limit: the Gmail connector routes any link in the body through a Google redirect page. The link text shown is still the real address. Links inside attachments and in the Gmail signature stay direct.
- Paid offers: answer "We are not interested in buying links or paid placements. We just want to share high-quality content that engages your audience."
- Founder features (CEO and founder magazines): his wording is "We are a startup, looking to improve our brand VTurnU ... please help us to publish about our founder and CEO. We will add the link with dofollow on our website and share it on our social media. Please help startups like us."
- Never pay, never swap links between third-party sites, never hide that VTurnU is his company.

- beehiiv, Hashnode (vturnu.hashnode.dev) and Indie Hackers (@bvignesh). The cloud session cannot log in to these.

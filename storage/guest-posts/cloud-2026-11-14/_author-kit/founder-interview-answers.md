## Third-person bio (IdeaMensch asks for 200 to 500 words)

B. Vignesh is the Founder & CEO of VTurnU, a B2B digital marketing and web development company based in Chennai, India. He began his career in digital marketing in 2017 and has worked across search, paid media, social media and websites ever since. In 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, he started VTurnU, and in 2025 he incorporated the company as VTurnU Digital Solutions LLP.

At VTurnU, Vignesh leads the company's work on search engine optimisation, AI search visibility, paid media, social media and websites that turn visits into qualified enquiries, for businesses across industries worldwide. He describes himself as an omni-channel marketer: every channel should serve one business goal, and the results should be judged by qualified leads and revenue, not by traffic alone.

He writes about how buyers now find companies, through search engines and through AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Copilot and Google's AI Overviews, and about what businesses can do to be found and trusted in both. He believes AI will keep making research, analysis and optimisation faster, while strategy, creativity, customer understanding and decision-making stay with people.

His approach to marketing is practical: understand the customer's questions, give accurate and useful answers, measure what reaches the sales pipeline, and let the budget follow results.

## Answers that can be given from published facts and views

- What does your company do? VTurnU is a B2B digital marketing and web development company in Chennai, India. We help businesses get found in search and in AI answers, and turn website visits into qualified enquiries.
- When did you start? I began my career in digital marketing in 2017, started VTurnU in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic and incorporated it in 2025.
- Which numbers show that marketing is working? Qualified leads, conversion rates, customer acquisition cost and revenue. Comparing qualified leads with traffic is especially important.
- What is one trend that excites you? AI search. Meaningful AI search optimization starts with understanding customer questions and providing accurate, useful answers.
- How will AI change agencies? AI will make research, analysis, content production and campaign optimization faster. Strategy, creativity, customer understanding and decision-making stay with people. Agencies that combine AI efficiency with human expertise will deliver greater value to their clients.
- What mistake do businesses make when choosing an agency? Choosing based only on low pricing or guaranteed rankings. Look for clear communication, relevant experience, transparent reporting and a practical understanding of your audience.
- One strategy that helps a business grow? One business goal that every channel serves, with conversion tracking and CRM data showing which channel produced revenue, so the budget follows results.
